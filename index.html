Alpha 0.1

Dev updates

Released October 25, 2026.

  • Smoother fights on phones: 31 → 35 fps, with a third as many stutters (89 → 30 a minute), on a 2019 Moto G7 Supra.
  • Quicker start: you're playing in 28 seconds instead of 31.
  • The first download is bigger: about 55 MB on phones, up from 32 MB.
  • Phones get a lighter Warrior (12k instead of 120k vertices); PCs keep full detail.
  • A new look for the Warrior, and a new HUD with his portrait and level.
  • Sweep now aims where your swipe starts, not just the way you flick: about 3.5 enemies hit per Sweep in our tests, up from about 1.
  • Hold your thumb on an enemy to lock on.
  • Power fills 3x faster, so you use your big attacks more often.
  • Snappier movement on touch: turns in 0.13 s instead of 0.33 s.
  • New higher camera, with a High/Low view option in Settings.
  • Fixed: crowd jostling no longer cancels your attacks.
